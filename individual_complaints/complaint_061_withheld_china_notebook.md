# IPCA COMPLAINT #061: WITHHOLDING EXCULPATORY EVIDENCE

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police withheld China Ambler's notebook containing the statement **"I need to stop lying"** - critical exculpatory evidence.

## THE SMOKING GUN

China's own notebook: "I need to stop lying"

This **proves consciousness of fabrication** and should have been disclosed to defense.

## VIOLATION

- Brady violation (suppression of exculpatory evidence)
- Breach of disclosure obligations
- Perverting course of justice
- Denial of fair trial

## EVIDENCE

- Notebook (if obtained)
- Discovery records showing withholding
- Trial transcripts showing evidence wasn't disclosed
- Violation of prosecution disclosure duty

## REQUESTED: Criminal prosecution for evidence suppression

## COMPENSATION: $75,000-$125,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #061**
