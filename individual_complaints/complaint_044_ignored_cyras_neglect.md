# IPCA COMPLAINT #044: IGNORED CYRAS REPORT - CHILD NEGLECT

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

- Multiple CYRAS reports
- Documented neglect concerns
- Police refusal to investigate

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series
