# IPCA COMPLAINT #054: CONTINUATION AFTER 7-MINUTE ACQUITTAL

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Jury deliberated only **7 minutes** before NOT GUILTY verdict, proving charges were obviously baseless from the start.

## THE 7-MINUTE DELIBERATION

**Context:** Average jury deliberation is hours or days.
**This case:** 7 minutes.
**Meaning:** Charges were so obviously false that minimal discussion was needed.

## VIOLATION

- Prosecution of obviously baseless charges
- Wasting court resources
- Malicious prosecution
- Harassment through criminal system

## EVIDENCE

- 7-minute jury deliberation (court records)
- NOT GUILTY verdict
- Remarkably short deliberation proving obvious falsity


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #054**
