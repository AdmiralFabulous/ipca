# IPCA COMPLAINT #051: FAILURE TO INVESTIGATE MOTIVE TO FABRICATE

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police failed to investigate obvious motive to fabricate: custody warfare and Family Court advantage.

## THE OBVIOUS MOTIVE

**China's own admission (Sept 12, 2023):**
"I filed a protection order against Alex so now it's a lot harder for him to get any custody rights"

This was **tactical litigation advantage**, not genuine safety concern.

## TIMING PROVES MOTIVE

- Pregnancy/custody concerns
- Protection order 64 days before Elliott's birth
- Criminal allegations coinciding with Family Court
- Facebook celebration of tactical success

## VIOLATION

- Failure to investigate motive
- Enabling weaponization of criminal system
- Malicious prosecution
- Custody warfare facilitation

## COMPENSATION: $40,000-$60,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #051**
