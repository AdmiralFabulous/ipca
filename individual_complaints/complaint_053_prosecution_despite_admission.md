# IPCA COMPLAINT #053: PROSECUTION DESPITE CHINA'S ADMISSION

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police continued prosecution despite China Ambler admitting emojis were "likely accidental" in police job sheet.

## THE SMOKING GUN

**Police job sheet:** China admitted emojis were "likely accidental"

Yet police prosecuted anyway.

## VIOLATION

- Malicious prosecution
- Continuing despite exculpatory admission
- Abuse of process
- Ignoring complainant's own admission

## EVIDENCE

- Police job sheet with admission
- Prosecution continued regardless
- 7-minute acquittal


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series
