# IPCA COMPLAINT #049: INADEQUATE INVESTIGATION BEFORE CHARGING

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police failed to properly investigate before filing 7 serious charges, leading to malicious prosecution and complete acquittal.

## INVESTIGATION FAILURES

- No independent corroboration sought
- No medical evidence (despite assault/strangulation claims)
- No forensic evidence
- No witness statements
- Sole reliance on China's allegations

## VIOLATION

- Prosecutorial misconduct
- Failure to investigate adequately
- Solicitor General's Prosecution Guidelines breach
- Malicious prosecution

## EVIDENCE

- Complete acquittal proving inadequate basis
- Lack of investigation records
- No corroborating evidence


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #049**
