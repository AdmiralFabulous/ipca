# IPCA COMPLAINT #016: RELIGIOUS DISCRIMINATION AND PERSECUTION

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Officer Sarah Perano targeted my company "199 Proverbs" (reference to Proverbs 19:9 - "A false witness will not go unpunished"), demonstrating religious discrimination.

## VIOLATION

- Religious discrimination
- Human Rights Act 1993 violation
- Persecution for religious expression
- Targeting based on Biblical reference

## EVIDENCE

- Company registration: "199 Proverbs"
- Biblical reference: Proverbs 19:9
- Officer's targeting of religious expression
- Pattern of harassment related to faith


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #016**
