## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

I am filing a formal complaint against New Zealand Police officers for the most severe **psychological torture**: telling me my unborn son Elliott had been aborted, causing me to believe my child was dead for **153 days** (approximately 5 months).

This is one of the most extreme forms of psychological torture possible - deliberately causing a parent to believe their child is dead. I only learned in September 2021 via email that Elliott had been born alive.

This constitutes **torture** under the UN Convention Against Torture Article 1.
