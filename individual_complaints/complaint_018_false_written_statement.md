# IPCA COMPLAINT #018: FALSE WRITTEN STATEMENT

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Constable James Peck falsely claimed in official written statement that I "punched China in the face." This never happened.

## VIOLATION

- Perjury (Crimes Act Section 110)
- Fabricating evidence
- False statement in official document
- Maximum penalty: 7 years imprisonment

## EVIDENCE

- Police written statement by Peck
- **Audio recording proving claim false**
- Lack of medical/photographic evidence
- Complete acquittal at trial

## REQUESTED: Criminal prosecution of Constable Peck

## COMPENSATION: $50,000-$100,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #018**
