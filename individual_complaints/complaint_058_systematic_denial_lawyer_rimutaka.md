# IPCA COMPLAINT #058: SYSTEMATIC DENIAL OF LAWYER ACCESS - RIMUTAKA

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Systematic denial and delays of lawyer access while detained at Rimutaka Prison.

## VIOLATION

- NZBORA Section 23(1)(b) violation
- Systematic blocking of counsel access
- Pattern of denials
- Violation of right to legal representation

## EVIDENCE

- Contemporaneous accounts
- Lawyer correspondence about access difficulties
- Rimutaka detention records
- Pattern of systematic denial


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #058**
