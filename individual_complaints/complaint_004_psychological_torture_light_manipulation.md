## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

I am filing a formal complaint against New Zealand Police officers for **psychological torture through light manipulation** during my 2021 detention. Officers deliberately:

1. Turned lights on and off repeatedly in my cell
2. Used systematic manipulation of lighting conditions
3. Applied deliberate psychological pressure to break me down
4. Disrupted sleep and caused disorientation

This constitutes **psychological torture** under the UN Convention Against Torture.

## DETAILED VIOLATION

### What Happened

During detention, officers:

1. **Systematically manipulated** cell lighting
2. **Turned lights on/off repeatedly**
3. **Deliberate psychological pressure** technique
4. **Sleep disruption** and disorientation tactics

### Legal Context

Light manipulation is **recognized form of psychological torture**:
- Used to disorient and break down detainees
- Interferes with sleep and mental state
- Creates extreme psychological distress
- Internationally condemned as torture technique

### Evidence Available

- My contemporaneous account
- Cell lighting control records (if maintained)
- Other detainee experiences in same facility
- Medical records showing psychological impact

### Rights Violated

**UN Convention Against Torture Article 16:** Prohibition of cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment
**NZBORA Section 9:** Right not to be subjected to cruel, degrading treatment
**UN Mandela Rules 42-43:** Lighting and cell conditions standards

### Impact

- Severe psychological distress
- Sleep deprivation
- Mental disorientation
- Inability to think clearly about legal situation
- Lasting emotional trauma
