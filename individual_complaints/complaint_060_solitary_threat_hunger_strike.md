# IPCA COMPLAINT #060: SOLITARY CONFINEMENT THREAT FOR DEMANDING LAWYER

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

When I threatened hunger strike to demand lawyer access, police/prison threatened me with solitary confinement.

## VIOLATION

- Retaliation for asserting legal rights
- Threatening punishment for demanding counsel
- Coercion to abandon right to lawyer
- Cruel and degrading treatment threat

## EVIDENCE

- Contemporaneous account of threat
- Context of denied lawyer access
- Hunger strike threat as last resort
- Solitary confinement retaliation threat

## COMPENSATION: $30,000-$50,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #060**
