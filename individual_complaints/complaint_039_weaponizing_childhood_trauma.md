# IPCA COMPLAINT #039: WEAPONIZING CHILDHOOD TRAUMA ALLEGATIONS

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Detective Bell weaponized China Ambler's false claim that I was "sexually abused 15 times" as child, using alleged childhood trauma against me.

## VIOLATION

- Weaponizing (false) child abuse allegations
- Stigmatizing abuse victims
- Forcing public disclosure of private matters
- No verification of claims
- Malicious use of trauma

## EVIDENCE

- Bell's letter citing childhood abuse claims
- No medical/therapy records
- Pure speculation from unreliable source
- Designed to suggest "mental health issues"

## COMPENSATION: $40,000-$60,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series
