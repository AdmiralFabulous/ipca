# IPCA COMPLAINT #036: ABUSE OF PRIVACY ACT PRINCIPLES

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Detective Bell misused Privacy Act Principle 11(e)(i) to justify disclosure of unverified allegations to Immigration NZ.

## VIOLATION

- Privacy Act 2020 misuse
- Principle 11(e)(i) does NOT authorize disclosure of:
  - Unverified allegations as facts
  - Non-existent mental health issues
  - Information designed to harm
  - Speculation while presumption of innocence applies

## EVIDENCE

- Bell's letter citing Principle 11(e)(i)
- Unverified nature of allegations
- Admission no diagnosis existed
- Improper disclosure purpose


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #036**
