# IPCA COMPLAINT #057: RETALIATION FOR CRITICIZING DELAYS

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police facilitated judicial retaliation for my legitimate criticism of Family Court procedural delays.

## THE "CRIME"

Criticizing that Family Court was taking too long to process matters.

## POLICE RESPONSE

Accepting referral and treating legitimate criticism as police matter.

## VIOLATION

- Facilitating retaliation
- Chilling effect on court criticism
- Denial of access to justice
- NZBORA Section 14 (freedom of expression) violation


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #057**
