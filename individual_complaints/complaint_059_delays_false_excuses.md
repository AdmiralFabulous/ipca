# IPCA COMPLAINT #059: DELAYS WITH FALSE EXCUSES

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Prison/police gave various false excuses for delays in lawyer access: "shift changes," "scheduling issues," etc.

## VIOLATION

- Pretextual excuses to deny counsel
- No legitimate basis for delays
- Violation of right to timely access
- Pattern of obstruction

## EVIDENCE

- Multiple excuses documented
- Pattern of delays
- No legitimate scheduling conflicts
- Systematic obstruction


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #059**
