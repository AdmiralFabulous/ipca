# IPCA COMPLAINT #050: FAILURE TO INVESTIGATE CHINA'S CREDIBILITY

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police failed to investigate China Ambler's credibility before filing charges, despite red flags.

## RED FLAGS IGNORED

- May 24, 2021 Facebook messages discussing charges
- July 14, 2021 celebration of "tactical" protection order
- Pattern of false allegations
- Motive: custody warfare
- Admission protection order was "to make custody harder"

## VIOLATION

- Failure to investigate complainant credibility
- Ignoring motive to fabricate
- Prosecution based on unreliable witness
- Enabling false allegations

## EVIDENCE

- Facebook messages May-July 2021
- Family Court transcript Sept 12, 2023 (admission)
- **Two jury acquittals**
- Credibility "comprehensively destroyed"


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #050**
