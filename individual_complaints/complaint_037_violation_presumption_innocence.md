# IPCA COMPLAINT #037: VIOLATION OF PRESUMPTION OF INNOCENCE

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Detective Bell contacted Immigration while criminal charges were **pending** (before trial), treating accusations as established facts.

## TIMELINE

- April 11, 2021: Charges filed
- **May 13, 2021:** Bell wrote to Immigration (32 days later)
- March-April 2025: Trial → **NOT GUILTY**
- September 2025: Trial → **NOT GUILTY**

## VIOLATION

- NZBORA Section 25(c) violation (presumption of innocence)
- Attempted immigration consequences **before trial**
- Circumventing criminal process
- Treating charges as proof of guilt

## EVIDENCE

- Timeline showing pre-trial contact
- Complete acquittals proving innocence
- No conviction ever obtained

## COMPENSATION: $75,000-$100,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #037**
