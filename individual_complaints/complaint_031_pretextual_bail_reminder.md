# IPCA COMPLAINT #031: HARASSMENT THROUGH PRETEXTUAL BAIL "REMINDER"

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police stopped me at airport to "remind" me of bail conditions while I was in **full compliance** and traveling to court-ordered supervised contact.

## VIOLATION

- Harassment through pretextual stop
- Abuse of bail monitoring powers
- NZBORA Section 23(5) (arbitrary detention)
- No legitimate law enforcement purpose

## EVIDENCE

- Full compliance with bail conditions
- Travel to supervised contact (lawful purpose)
- Security officer witness
- No breach alleged or suspected

## COMPENSATION: $20,000-$35,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #031**
