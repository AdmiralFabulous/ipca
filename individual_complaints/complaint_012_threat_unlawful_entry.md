# IPCA COMPLAINT #012: THREAT TO UNLAWFULLY ENTER HOME WITHOUT WARRANT

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority  
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

On June 3, 2023, Officer Sarah Perano explicitly threatened: **"I will unlawfully enter your home without a warrant if I have to"**

This was a direct threat to violate my rights under NZBORA.

## VIOLATION

- Threat of unlawful entry
- Officer **admitted** entry would be "unlawful"
- Threatened to proceed anyway
- Coercion through illegal threats
- Violation of NZBORA Section 21

## EVIDENCE

- Contemporaneous account of threat
- Possible body camera footage
- Witness statements


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #012**
