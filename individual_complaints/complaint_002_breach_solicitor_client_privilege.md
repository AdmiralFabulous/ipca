## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

I am filing a formal complaint against New Zealand Police officers for **breaching solicitor-client privilege** during my 2021 arrest. During my supposedly "privileged" consultation with duty solicitor:

1. Arresting officer remained standing in/near doorway of consultation room
2. Officer could overhear conversation between myself and solicitor
3. No private consultation provided despite legal requirement

This violated fundamental legal professional privilege and NZBORA Section 23(1)(b).
