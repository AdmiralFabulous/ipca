# IPCA COMPLAINT #034: INTERFERENCE WITH PARENTAL RIGHTS

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police stopped me while traveling to court-ordered supervised contact with my son, disrupting precious limited time.

## IMPACT

- Delayed arrival to contact
- Stress before seeing Elliott
- I have had contact for only **0.237% of Elliott's life**
- Every minute is precious
- Police deliberately disrupted parental contact

## VIOLATION

- Care of Children Act 2004 violation
- Interference with court orders
- NZBORA family relationship protection
- Denial of parental rights


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #034**
