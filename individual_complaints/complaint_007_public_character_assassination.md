# IPCA COMPLAINT #007: PUBLIC CHARACTER ASSASSINATION TO NEIGHBORS

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Officer Sarah Perano shouted derogatory things about my character to my neighbors on January 10, 2022, causing public humiliation and reputational damage.

## AUDIO EVIDENCE

"SARAH CONTINUES TO SHOUT DEROGATORY THINGS ABOUT MY CHARACTER TO MY NEIGHBOURS"

## VIOLATION

- Public defamation
- Abuse of police authority to damage reputation
- Harassment through public character assassination
- Privacy violations

## EVIDENCE

- Audio transcript showing shouting to neighbors
- Neighbor witness testimony
- Pattern of harassment

## COMPENSATION: $25,000-$50,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #007**
