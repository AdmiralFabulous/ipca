# IPCA COMPLAINT #052: FILING BASELESS PROTECTION ORDER BREACH CHARGES

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police filed 2 protection order breach charges for **accidental thumbs-up emojis** that China admitted were "likely accidental."

## THE CHARGES

2 protection order breaches for emoji reactions.

## VERDICT: NOT GUILTY ON BOTH CHARGES

**Jury deliberation: 7 MINUTES** - remarkably short, indicating obviously baseless.

## VIOLATION

- Malicious prosecution
- Filing charges for trivial matter
- Prosecution despite complainant's admission
- Abuse of criminal system

## EVIDENCE

- 7-minute jury deliberation
- Police job sheet: China said "likely accidental"
- Complete acquittal
- Trivial nature of alleged breach

## COMPENSATION: $50,000-$75,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #052**
