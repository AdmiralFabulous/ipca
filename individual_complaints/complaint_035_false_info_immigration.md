# IPCA COMPLAINT #035: FALSE INFORMATION TO IMMIGRATION NZ

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Detective Jocelyn Bell wrote to Immigration NZ (May 13, 2021) providing false information about "mental health issues" to cause my deportation.

## THE SMOKING GUN

Bell's own letter: **"any mental health issues he may have may not yet have been diagnosed"**

Translation: No evidence exists. Pure speculation. Yet she still wrote to Immigration.

## VIOLATION

- Crimes Act Section 113 (fabricating evidence)
- Providing false information to government agency
- Attempted deportation on lies
- No diagnosis existed (Bell admitted this)

## EVIDENCE

- Detective Bell's May 13, 2021 letter
- Admission: "may not yet have been diagnosed"
- Complete acquittals proving allegations false
- No medical records supporting claims

## REQUESTED: Criminal prosecution of Detective Bell

## COMPENSATION: $100,000-$150,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #035**
