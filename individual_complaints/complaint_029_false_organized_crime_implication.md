# IPCA COMPLAINT #029: FALSE IMPLICATION OF ORGANIZED CRIME

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

OCU presence at my home created false public perception that I am connected to organized crime, gangs, or trafficking.

## VIOLATION

- Defamation by implication
- Reputational damage
- No organized crime connection
- Abuse of police powers for defamation
- Privacy Act violation

## EVIDENCE

- OCU deployment for non-organized crime matter
- Neighbor observations
- Community perception damage
- No legitimate justification


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #029**
