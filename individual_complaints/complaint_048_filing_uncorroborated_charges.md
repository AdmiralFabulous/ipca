# IPCA COMPLAINT #048: FILING UNCORROBORATED CHARGES - WELLINGTON

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Police filed **7 serious charges** based solely on uncorroborated allegations from discredited complainant China Ambler.

## THE 7 CHARGES

All based on China's word alone:
- Assault
- Strangulation
- Suffocation
- Assault with weapon
- Indecent assault
- Sexual violation x2

## VERDICT: NOT GUILTY ON ALL 7 CHARGES

## VIOLATION

- Malicious prosecution
- Filing charges without corroboration
- Inadequate investigation
- Reliance on single uncorroborated source

## EVIDENCE

- Trial verdict: NOT GUILTY x7
- Lack of corroborating evidence
- 2-hour jury deliberation
- Lawyer: "credibility comprehensively destroyed"


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #048**
