## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

I am filing a formal complaint against New Zealand Police officers for **denying my right to chosen legal counsel** during my 2021 arrest. Officers deliberately:

1. Falsely informed me I was required to select from duty solicitor list only
2. Lied about my lawyer's availability without attempting contact
3. Forced appointment of duty solicitor Mike Antanovich against my explicit wishes

This violated NZBORA Section 23(1)(b) - the right to consult and instruct a lawyer without delay.

## DETAILED VIOLATION

### What Happened

Upon arrest, I requested to contact my own lawyer. Police officers:

1. **Falsely claimed** I must use duty solicitor list
2. **Lied** that my lawyer was unavailable (no attempt to contact made)
3. **Forced appointment** of Mike Antanovich
4. **Refused** to facilitate contact with my chosen counsel

### Evidence Available

- My contemporaneous statements requesting own lawyer
- Duty solicitor appointment records showing non-voluntary selection
- No records of attempted contact with my chosen lawyer
- Recording of consultation (if available)

### Rights Violated

**NZBORA Section 23(1)(b):** Right to consult and instruct a lawyer without delay
**NZBORA Section 24(c):** Right to receive legal assistance without delay

### Impact

- Forced to consult with lawyer I did not trust or choose
- Defense compromised from the beginning
- Denied effective legal representation during critical early questioning
- Psychological distress from being denied fundamental rights
