# IPCA COMPLAINT #013: BREACH OF SOLICITOR-CLIENT PRIVILEGE - PHONE

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Officer Sarah Perano read or accessed privileged communications with my lawyer on my seized mobile phone, June 3, 2023.

## AUDIO EVIDENCE

**Oppo watch recording (July 4, 2023)** at approximately 1 hour mark: admission of phone seizure and access.

## VIOLATION

- Legal professional privilege breach
- Violation of Search and Surveillance Act Section 137
- NZBORA Section 21 violation
- Privacy Act 2020 breach
- Absolute protection of client communications violated

## EVIDENCE

- Oppo watch recording July 4, 2023
- Phone records showing lawyer communications
- Metadata showing access times

## COMPENSATION: $75,000-$125,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #013**
