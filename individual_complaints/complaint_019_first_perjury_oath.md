# IPCA COMPLAINT #019: FIRST PERJURY UNDER OATH

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Constable James Peck testified under oath that I said "I punched China in the face" during phone call. **Audio recording proves this is false.**

## VIOLATION

- Perjury (Crimes Act Section 108)
- False testimony in judicial proceeding
- Material false statement
- Maximum penalty: 7 years imprisonment

## EVIDENCE

- Court transcript of perjured testimony
- **Phone audio recording disproving claim**
- Complete acquittal proving perjury
