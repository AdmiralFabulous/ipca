# IPCA COMPLAINT #015: STRATEGIC ARREST TIMING - FAMILY COURT INTERFERENCE

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Arrest on **June 3, 2023** occurred on the **exact day** I was changing my Family Court contact supervisor, deliberately interfering with parenting arrangements.

## VIOLATION

- Abuse of arrest powers for collateral purposes
- Interference with Family Court orders
- Violation of Care of Children Act 2004
- Deliberate disruption of parent-child contact

## EVIDENCE

- Family Court orders showing June 3, 2023 supervisor change
- Timeline proving exact date correlation
- Pattern of police interference with parenting


**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #015**
