# IPCA COMPLAINT #056: MISUSE OF SECTION 11B FAMILY COURT ACT

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Judge Hambleton weaponized Section 11B Family Court Act to refer legitimate court criticism to police as "threatening."

## VIOLATION

- Abuse of judicial power
- Misuse of Section 11B
- No genuine threat existed
- Weaponization of statute
- Police facilitated judicial retaliation

## EVIDENCE

- Section 11B referral
- Memorandum content (non-threatening)
- Legitimate criticism context
- Police acceptance of improper referral

## COMPENSATION: $25,000-$40,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series

**END OF COMPLAINT #056**
