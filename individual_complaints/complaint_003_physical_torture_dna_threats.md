## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

I am filing a formal complaint against New Zealand Police officers for **physical torture through DNA extraction threats** during my 2021 arrest. Officers threatened me with:

1. Explicit threats of violence if I did not comply with DNA sampling
2. Threats of physical force to take samples against my will
3. Coercion through intimidation rather than lawful process

This constitutes **torture** under the UN Convention Against Torture and violates NZBORA Section 9.

## DETAILED VIOLATION

### What Happened

Police officers threatened me to compel DNA sample:

1. **Explicit threats** of violence if I did not comply
2. **Threats of physical force** to extract samples against will
3. **Coercion and intimidation** rather than lawful court-ordered process

### Legal Context

Under Criminal Investigations (Bodily Samples) Act 1995:
- DNA samples may only be taken with **consent** OR **court-authorized compulsion order**
- Threats of violence to obtain samples = **torture** and unlawful coercion
- Any sample obtained under duress is **inadmissible**

### Evidence Available

- My contemporaneous account of threats
- Any recording of DNA sampling process
- Witness statements if others present
- Medical records documenting psychological trauma

### Rights Violated

**UN Convention Against Torture Article 1:** Intentionally inflicting severe pain/suffering to obtain information
**NZBORA Section 9:** Right not to be subjected to torture or cruel, degrading treatment
**NZBORA Section 23(4):** Right to refrain from making any statement

### Impact

- Provided DNA under extreme duress and fear of violence
- Psychological trauma from threats
- Violation of bodily autonomy
- Any DNA evidence obtained should be inadmissible
