# IPCA COMPLAINT #027: MISUSE OF ORGANISED CRIME UNIT RESOURCES

**To:** Independent Police Conduct Authority
**Email:** info@ipca.govt.nz

## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Organised Crime Unit - NZ's elite unit for gangs, trafficking, cartels - was deployed to "interview" me about an **emoji** in legal correspondence.

## VIOLATION

- Gross misallocation of public resources
- Abuse of serious crime unit for trivial matter
- No legitimate basis for OCU involvement
- Intimidation through disproportionate response

## EVIDENCE

- OCU presence for "emoji" matter
- No organized crime connection
- Legal correspondence context
- Disproportionate response proving harassment intent

## COMPENSATION: $40,000-$60,000

**Date:** November 16, 2025
**Part of:** 61-complaint series
