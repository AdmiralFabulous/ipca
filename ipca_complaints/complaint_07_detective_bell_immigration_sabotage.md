### VIOLATION 2: ABUSE OF PRIVACY ACT PRINCIPLES

**What Happened:**
Detective Bell cited **"Principle 11(e)(i) of the Privacy Principles"** to justify her disclosure to Immigration.

**Privacy Act 2020, Principle 11(e)(i) allows disclosure:**
> "Where the disclosure is necessary to enable an agency to carry out its functions."

**Bell's Misuse:**

**This principle does NOT authorize:**
- Disclosure of **unverified allegations** as facts
- Disclosure of **non-existent mental health issues**
- Disclosure designed to **harm** rather than inform
- Disclosure while **presumption of innocence** applies

**Proper Use of Principle 11(e)(i):**
- Disclosure of **verified information** relevant to immigration decision
- Disclosure of **convictions** (not mere charges)
- Disclosure of **actual threats** to public safety

**Bell's Improper Use:**
- Disclosure of **unproven allegations**
- Disclosure of **non-existent diagnoses**
- Disclosure designed to **cause deportation**
- Disclosure while **charges pending** (not convicted)

**Legal Violation:**

Under **Privacy Act 2020:**
- Misuse of privacy principles is unlawful
- Agencies must use information fairly and lawfully
- Cannot disclose unverified allegations to cause harm
