## OBSTRUCTION OF JUSTICE

The deletion of complaints constitutes **obstruction of justice**:

**Under Crimes Act 1961, Section 116:**
"Every one is liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years who, with intent to obstruct, prevent, pervert, or defeat the course of justice, does any act or omits to do any act."

**Christchurch Police:**
1. **Deleted official complaints** about serious criminal conduct
2. **Prevented investigation** of perjury and blackmail
3. **Destroyed records** of criminal allegations
4. **Protected alleged offender** from accountability
5. **Obstructed justice** by preventing prosecution

This is particularly egregious because:
- Police are supposed to **facilitate** justice, not obstruct it
- Perjury **directly undermines** the justice system
- Police had **duty to investigate**, not destroy evidence
- Deletion appears **deliberate** to protect false accuser
