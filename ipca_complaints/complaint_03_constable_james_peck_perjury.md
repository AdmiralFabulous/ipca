## DETAILED ACCOUNT OF PERJURY INSTANCES

### PERJURY INSTANCE 1: FALSE WRITTEN STATEMENT

**What Happened:**
Constable James Peck **falsely claimed in an official written police statement** that:

> **"[Complainant] punched China [Ambler] in the face"**

**The Truth:**
This never happened. I did not punch anyone in the face.

**Evidence:**
- Police written statement/report by Constable Peck
- **Audio recording from my phone** proving no such assault occurred
- Lack of any medical evidence of facial injury
- Lack of any photographs of facial injury
- China Ambler's own statements
- Complete acquittal at trial

**Legal Violation:**
Under **Crimes Act 1961 Section 110:**
- **Perjury** - making a false statement in an official document
- **Maximum penalty:** 7 years imprisonment
- **Aggravated** when committed by police officer in position of trust

**Impact of False Written Statement:**
1. I was arrested based on this false claim
2. I was charged with serious assault
3. Criminal prosecution was initiated
4. I spent years defending myself against false allegations
5. My reputation was damaged
6. My relationship with my child was severed

---

### PERJURY INSTANCE 2: FIRST TESTIMONY UNDER OATH

**What Happened:**
When called to testify in court, Constable James Peck **repeated the false claim under oath**:

> **Testified that I punched China in the face**

**Evidence:**
- Court transcript of Peck's testimony
- Audio recording proving the claim is false
- Cross-examination records

**Legal Violation:**
Under **Crimes Act 1961 Section 108(1):**
> "Every one is liable to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years who, on any judicial proceeding... makes a statement material in that proceeding, which he knows to be false."

Constable Peck:
1. **Testified under oath** (judicial proceeding)
2. **Made a material statement** (alleged assault central to charges)
3. **Knew it was false** (no evidence, audio recording proves otherwise)
4. **Intended to secure conviction** (prosecution witness)

**Impact:**
- Perjured testimony used to prosecute me
- False evidence presented to court
- Jury nearly misled by police officer's lies
- Perversion of justice

### PERJURY INSTANCE 3: CHANGED STORY WHEN CAUGHT

**What Happened:**

**New False Claim:**

**The Truth:**
I did not say this either in person or on the phone. This was a **revised lie** to account for being caught in the first lie.

**Evidence:**
- Court transcript showing revised testimony
- Audio recording disproving phone call claim
- **In-person recording** also disproving revised claim
- Timeline proving impossibility of in-person statement

**Legal Analysis:**
This is **consciousness of guilt**:
1. Original lie exposed by evidence
2. Officer fabricated new lie to maintain prosecution
3. Demonstrated deliberate dishonesty, not honest mistake
4. Proves Peck knew his testimony was false

**Additional Perjury:**
The revised statement is **itself perjury** - a new false statement under oath attempting to cover up the first perjury.

Applies to fabricating evidence with intent to mislead in judicial proceedings.

Applies to conduct intended to obstruct, prevent, pervert, or defeat the course of justice.
