## VIOLATIONS

### VIOLATION 1: HARASSMENT THROUGH PRETEXTUAL STOP

**What Happened:**
Police used bail conditions as **pretext** to harass me when no enforcement action was needed.

**Evidence:**
- I was in full compliance with all conditions
- No breach alleged or suspected
- "Reminder" served no legitimate purpose
- I was already aware of conditions (obviously)

**Legal Analysis:**

**Bail Monitoring vs. Harassment:**

**Legitimate bail monitoring:**
- Checking compliance when there's reason to suspect breach
- Responding to reported violations
- Following up on specific concerns
- Proportionate to risk assessment

**Harassment:**
- Stopping someone in full compliance
- "Reminding" them of conditions they're following
- Using bail as excuse for unnecessary contact
- Public humiliation with no law enforcement purpose

**This Was Harassment Because:**
1. No breach suspected or alleged
2. No legitimate investigative purpose
3. Solely for intimidation and humiliation
4. Pattern of targeting me for lawful activity

**Rights Violated:**
- **NZBORA Section 23(5):** Right to be free from arbitrary detention
- **Bail Act 2000:** Misuse of bail monitoring powers
- **Freedom of movement:** Interference with lawful travel

## ABUSE OF BAIL MONITORING POWERS

### Legitimate vs. Illegitimate Use of Bail Conditions

**Bail Act 2000 Purpose:**
- Ensure attendance at court
- Prevent offending while on bail
- Protect victims and public
- Proportionate to risk

**Legitimate Bail Monitoring:**
- Responding to alleged breaches
- Following up on reports of non-compliance
- Checking compliance when specific concern arises
- Investigating suspected violations

**Illegitimate Use (This Case):**
- **Harassing compliant defendant**
- **Using conditions as excuse for contact**
- **Public humiliation** with no enforcement purpose
- **Interfering with lawful activity**
- **Targeted harassment** pattern

**This Was Abuse of Power Because:**
1. I was in **full compliance** with all conditions
2. No **breach alleged** or suspected
3. No **legitimate purpose** for contact
4. **Purely harassing** in nature
5. Part of **pattern** of targeting
