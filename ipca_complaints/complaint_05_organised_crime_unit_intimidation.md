**Legal Implications:**

Under **Crimes Act 1961, Section 71 (Accessory After the Fact):**
> "Every one is liable as an accessory after the fact to an offense who, knowing any person to have been a party to the offense, receives, comforts, or assists that person... in order to enable him to escape after arrest."

**Police officers who retaliate against complainants to protect judicial misconduct are:**
- Assisting officers (judges) who have committed offenses (abuse of power)
- Enabling them to escape accountability
- Obstructing justice

This creates **criminal liability** for officers involved in retaliation.
