## WHY THIS WAS FALSE IMPRISONMENT

### Definition of False Imprisonment

Under New Zealand law, **false imprisonment** occurs when:
1. Person is detained/imprisoned
2. Without lawful justification
3. Against their will

**All Three Elements Present:**

**1. I Was Detained:**
- Held in custody for 37 days
- Complete loss of liberty
- Could not leave detention facility

**2. Without Lawful Justification:**
- Charges were **completely false** (proven by acquittal)
- Based on **perjury** (Constable Peck's lies)
- Based on **fabricated evidence**
- **No reasonable grounds** for imprisonment (acquittal proves this)

**3. Against My Will:**
- Did not consent to imprisonment
- Maintained innocence throughout
- Complete acquittal vindicated my position
