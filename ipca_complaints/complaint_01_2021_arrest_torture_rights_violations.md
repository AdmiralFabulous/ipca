## COMPLAINT SUMMARY

I am filing a formal complaint against New Zealand Police officers involved in my first arrest in 2021 for multiple serious violations including:

1. Denial of right to chosen legal counsel
2. Breach of solicitor-client privilege

## DETAILED ACCOUNT OF VIOLATIONS

### VIOLATION 1: DENIAL OF RIGHT TO CHOSEN LEGAL COUNSEL

**What Happened:**
Upon arrest, I requested to contact my own lawyer. Police officers deliberately:

1. **Falsely informed me** that I was required to select from the duty solicitor list only
2. **Lied about my lawyer's availability**, claiming they were unavailable when no attempt was made to contact them
3. **Forced the appointment** of duty solicitor Mike Antanovich against my explicit wishes

**Evidence:**
- My contemporaneous statements during arrest
- Recording of consultation (if available)
- Duty solicitor appointment records showing I did not voluntarily choose Mr. Antanovich

**Rights Violated:**
- **NZBORA Section 23(1)(b):** Right to consult and instruct a lawyer without delay
- **NZBORA Section 24(c):** Right to receive legal assistance without delay

**Impact:**
- I was forced to consult with a lawyer I did not trust or choose
- My defense was compromised from the very beginning
- I was denied the opportunity to have effective legal representation during critical early questioning

---

### VIOLATION 2: BREACH OF SOLICITOR-CLIENT PRIVILEGE

**What Happened:**
During my supposedly "privileged" consultation with the duty solicitor:

1. **The arresting officer remained standing in or near the doorway** of the consultation room
2. **The officer could overhear** the conversation between myself and the duty solicitor
3. **No private consultation was provided** despite legal requirement for privilege

**Evidence:**
- My contemporaneous account of the consultation
- Consultation room layout and acoustics
- Officer's own notes/records showing presence during consultation period

**Rights Violated:**
- **Legal professional privilege** - fundamental common law right
- **NZBORA Section 23(1)(b):** Right to consult lawyer (implicitly requires privacy)
- **New Zealand Law Society Rules:** Client confidentiality requirements

**Impact:**
- Complete violation of solicitor-client privilege
- Police obtained information about my defense strategy
- My ability to receive frank legal advice was compromised
- I could not speak freely about my case

---

### VIOLATION 3: PHYSICAL TORTURE - DNA EXTRACTION THREATS

**What Happened:**
Police officers threatened me with **physical force** to compel DNA sample provision:

1. **Explicit threats of violence** if I did not comply with DNA sampling
2. **Threats of physical force** to take samples against my will
3. **Coercion through intimidation** rather than lawful process

**Evidence:**
- My contemporaneous account of threats
- Any recording of the DNA sampling process
- Witness statements if others were present

**Rights Violated:**
- **United Nations Convention Against Torture Article 1:** Torture defined as intentionally inflicting severe pain or suffering to obtain information or confession
- **NZBORA Section 9:** Right not to be subjected to torture or cruel, degrading, or disproportionately severe treatment
- **NZBORA Section 23(4):** Right to refrain from making any statement

**Legal Context:**
Under the Criminal Investigations (Bodily Samples) Act 1995:
- DNA samples may only be taken with consent OR by court-authorized compulsion order
- Threats of violence to obtain samples constitute torture and unlawful coercion
- Any sample obtained under duress is inadmissible

**Impact:**
- I provided DNA under extreme duress and fear of violence
- Psychological trauma from threats
- Violation of bodily autonomy
- Any DNA evidence obtained should be deemed inadmissible

### VIOLATION 4: PSYCHOLOGICAL TORTURE - LIGHT MANIPULATION

**What Happened:**
During my detention, police officers deliberately **turned the lights on and off repeatedly** in my cell:

1. **Systematic manipulation** of lighting conditions
2. **Deliberate psychological pressure** to break me down
3. **Sleep disruption** and disorientation tactics

**Evidence:**
- My contemporaneous account
- Cell lighting control records (if maintained)
- Other detainee experiences in same facility

**Rights Violated:**
- **United Nations Convention Against Torture Article 16:** Prohibition of cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment
- **NZBORA Section 9:** Right not to be subjected to cruel, degrading treatment
- **United Nations Standard Minimum Rules for the Treatment of Prisoners (Mandela Rules):** Rules 42-43 regarding lighting and cell conditions

**Legal Context:**
Light manipulation is a recognized form of psychological torture:
- Used to disorient and break down detainees
- Interferes with sleep and mental state
- Creates extreme psychological distress
- Internationally condemned as torture technique

**Impact:**
- Severe psychological distress
- Sleep deprivation
- Mental disorientation
- Inability to think clearly about my legal situation
- Emotional trauma lasting beyond detention

---

### VIOLATION 5: PSYCHOLOGICAL TORTURE - THE ABORTION LIE (153 DAYS OF ANGUISH)

**What Happened:**
Police officers informed me that **my partner had aborted our child, Elliott**:

1. **False information provided** about the termination of my unborn child
2. **I believed my son was dead** for **153 days** (approximately 5 months)
3. **Only learned the truth in September 2021** via email that Elliott had been born alive
4. **Deliberate infliction of severe emotional suffering**

**Evidence:**
- My contemporaneous grief and trauma
- Email dated September 2021 revealing Elliott's birth
- Timeline showing 153-day period of believing child was dead
- Birth certificate showing Elliott born September 14, 2021
- My statements and communications during this period reflecting belief child was deceased

**Rights Violated:**
- **United Nations Convention Against Torture Article 1:** Intentional infliction of severe mental suffering
- **NZBORA Section 9:** Right not to be subjected to torture or cruel, degrading treatment
- **NZBORA Section 11:** Right not to be subjected to medical or scientific experimentation (psychological experimentation)

**Legal Context:**
This constitutes one of the most severe forms of psychological torture:
- Deliberately causing a parent to believe their child is dead
- Maintaining this deception for 153 days
- Causing grief, anguish, and psychological devastation
- No legitimate law enforcement purpose
- Purely punitive and cruel

**Impact:**
- **153 days of grieving** for a child I believed was dead
- Severe emotional trauma and psychological suffering
- Depression and mental anguish
- Loss of critical time bonding with my newborn son
- **Prevented from being present at Elliott's birth** - I did not know he was being born
- **Never got to hold my son as a newborn** because I believed he didn't exist
- Ongoing psychological harm and trust issues
- This cruelty cannot be undone or compensated
